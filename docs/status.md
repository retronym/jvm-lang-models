# Status: what is proved, and what is checked

The models are specifications ([`docs/specification.md`](specification.md)): a compiler, a linker or a checker is an instance whose algorithm is a task with a trace. A **theorem** holds for every program; a **check** (an `example`, often by `native_decide`, or a probe against the real tool) holds on a bounded space. Each layer is calibrated against its real tool before theorems are built on it. The obligations and T1–T5 are stated in [`theorems.md`](theorems.md).

## Against the bug trackers

Against Zinc's bug tracker ([`docs/evidence/bug-map.md`](evidence/bug-map.md), counted from its rows): of 145 catalogued bugs, 56 are covered by an instance, 38 partially, and 50 are gaps, 35 of them compiler `comp` or snapshot-bookkeeping rows with no phase. An instance has a witness with the bug's trace for 52 and the family without this witness for 26; most instances of phases 15 to 28 were built after the bug, so these are not predictions. 16 pending and 3 disabled scripted tests are mapped the same way.

## Trust

No `sorry`, `admit` or `axiom` declarations. No `theorem` or `lemma` is proved by `native_decide`; the enumerated facts are `example`s. CI lints this over every `.lean` file ([`scripts/lint_native_decide.py`](../scripts/lint_native_decide.py)) and checks that 90 theorems (T1–T5 per framework variant, B4, and the instances' soundness theorems) use only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` ([`scripts/Axioms.lean`](../scripts/Axioms.lean), [`scripts/check_axioms.py`](../scripts/check_axioms.py)).
