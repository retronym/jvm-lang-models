# Phase records

One record per phase of the Zinc model from phase 10 on: the question, what the compiler reads, the design, and the results. Phases 1 to 9 are in [`docs/history/phases.md`](../../../docs/history/phases.md) at the repository root; what each phase proved and checked is in [`docs/status.md`](../../../docs/status.md).

[`28-sam.md`](28-sam.md) is the template for a new feature. The table says, per phase, what is proved for every program and what is only checked on a bounded space or by the conformance harness; phases 1 to 9 are written up in [`docs/history/phases.md`](../../../docs/history/phases.md), phases 4 and 5 were design notes whose results are in 6 to 8.
